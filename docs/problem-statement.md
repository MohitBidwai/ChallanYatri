# ChallanYatri — Problem Statement

## Context

Traffic challan services already allow citizens to look up challans and, depending on the applicable authority and process, pay or raise grievances. The challenge is not simply the absence of a grievance option.

The citizen experience can become fragmented when a person believes that a challan is incorrect and needs to understand what happened, determine the appropriate way to challenge it, submit the required information successfully, and know what happens after submission.

## The Problem

> **When a citizen believes a traffic challan has been issued incorrectly, the process to understand the challan, identify the appropriate way to challenge it, submit a dispute successfully, and track what happens afterward can be fragmented and difficult to navigate.**

The core question for the citizen is:

> **“I believe this challan is wrong. Now what?”**

## Real-World Motivation

The product was inspired by a real user experience in which a traffic challan was believed to be incorrect and the attempt to raise a grievance did not successfully resolve the issue.

This experience highlights an important distinction:

> **Having a grievance option is not enough. Citizens need a clear and reliable journey from disagreement to submission to resolution tracking.**

The prototype must describe this as a user-reported problem rather than making an unsupported claim that a particular authority or officer acted improperly.

## What We Are Solving

ChallanYatri focuses on the **dispute and resolution journey**, not on replacing the entire government e-Challan ecosystem.

The product aims to make it easier for a citizen to:

1. Find the relevant challan.
2. Understand the alleged violation.
3. Review the available evidence/details.
4. Decide whether to pay or dispute.
5. Explain why they believe the challan is incorrect.
6. Provide supporting information/evidence.
7. Receive confirmation that their dispute was recorded in the prototype.
8. Track the dispute lifecycle and understand what happens next.

## Target User

The primary user is an Indian vehicle owner who has received a traffic challan and believes that the challan may be incorrect or requires clarification.

## Non-Goals

ChallanYatri will **not** attempt to:

- replace the official e-Challan system;
- become an official government grievance portal;
- determine the legal validity of a challan;
- make a real government complaint submission;
- process real payments;
- guarantee a dispute outcome;
- cover every state/authority-specific workflow in the MVP.

## Product Hypothesis

If citizens are given one clear, transparent journey that explains the challan, presents their available options, guides them through a dispute, confirms submission, and provides understandable status tracking, then the process of challenging an incorrect challan can become less confusing and more trustworthy.

## Prototype Transparency

ChallanYatri is an independent prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government department or traffic authority.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, challan information, payment outcomes, authority review, dispute outcomes, and status updates shown in the prototype are simulated for demonstration purposes.
